## Design Context

Derived from `DESIGN.md`, which stays the source of truth for `apps/petabase` (tokens, shell behavior, breakpoints). If the two disagree, follow `DESIGN.md` and update this file.

### Users
- Veterinary clinic staff running daily operations in Petabase (staff-facing, not consumer-facing).
- Two main contexts: desktop at the front desk, and mobile on the clinic floor.
- Long working sessions over dense patient, appointment and billing data; the interface must keep cognitive load low.
- Bilingual Thai/English from the start.

### Brand Personality
- Three words: calm, clinical, trustworthy.
- Not playful and not consumer-social.
- Emotional goal: comfort over long sessions and confidence that the data is right.

### Aesthetic Direction
- Visual language: **Calm Ops**. White surfaces, light borders, soft blue-tinted elevation, a single violet primary (`--pb-color-primary` `#533afd`).
- Typography: Plus Jakarta Sans for Latin/UI, Noto Sans Thai for Thai, Source Code Pro for mono. Numbers stay in the Latin stack in Thai locale for tabular clarity.
- Device-native shell by breakpoint: Mac-like workspace on desktop, iPad-like top nav on tablet, iPhone-like bottom quick actions on mobile.
- Motion is short and purposeful (~140-180ms) with a small stagger fade-up for card entry.
- Anti-references: marketing-hero styling, decorative visual noise, hidden-only navigation, ad hoc fonts.
- Theme: light.

### Design Principles
1. Data first: nothing decorative competes with patient, appointment or billing data.
2. Use the tokens in `apps/petabase/src/styles/tokens.css`; no one-off colors, radii or shadows.
3. Keep orientation in long sessions: persistent navigation, never hidden-only patterns.
4. Bilingual readability first, including Thai line-height and numeric clarity.
5. Accessible and touch-safe: visible focus states, contrast in every state, key actions one tap away on mobile with safe-area handling.
